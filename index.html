What is Seedream 5.5?

  
Seedream 5.5 is ByteDance's image generation model, released on November 9, 2026 and rolled out the same week to Dreamina, CapCut, BytePlus and Doubao. It generates up to 2K resolution images and is built for design work rather than just pretty pictures:

  
    
    
  • Exact text rendering — put words in quotation marks and 5.5 prints them letter-for-letter, which makes posters, packaging and ads practical.
    • 
    
  • Transparent PNG output — ask for a transparent background and you get clean cut-outs for stickers, logos and overlays.
    • 
    
  • Up to 10 reference images — feed it product shots or a character from several angles and it keeps the subject consistent.
    • 
    
  • Iterative editing — the same image can be refined over many rounds of edits without the subject drifting.
    •

Seedream 5.5 prompt-writing tips

  
    
    
  • Quote your text. Text inside "double quotes" is rendered verbatim. Unquoted text is treated as a description and often comes out misspelled.
    • 
    
  • One subject, one scene. A prompt built around a single clear subject beats a collage of ideas — generate variants separately and composite later.
    • 
    
  • Use references for consistency. For a product line or a recurring character, upload reference images and say what must stay identical (shape, color, logo placement).
    • 
    
  • Say "transparent background" out loud. For stickers and logos, explicitly ask for a transparent PNG background — don't rely on "no background" phrasing alone.
    • 
    
  • Iterate in small steps. 5.5 is designed for long editing chains: change one thing per round and the image stays coherent far longer than with regenerate-from-scratch workflows.
    • 
    
  • State the format. Mention aspect and use ("a vertical poster", "a square product shot") in the prompt when the app doesn't expose size controls.

FAQ

  

    Is this prompt generator free?
    
Yes. It runs entirely in your browser, needs no account, and nothing you type is sent anywhere or stored. Generating the actual image happens in your Seedream 5.5 app (Dreamina, CapCut, BytePlus, Doubao), which may have its own pricing.

  

  

    How do I get Seedream 5.5 to spell text correctly?
    
Put the exact words in quotation marks in the prompt — for example, a poster with the text "SUMMER SALE". Seedream 5.5 treats quoted text as literal content to print, not as a topic to illustrate. Keep quoted text short; long paragraphs still trip up every image model.

  

  

    Where can I use Seedream 5.5?
    
Seedream 5.5 launched inside ByteDance's own products — Dreamina, CapCut, BytePlus and Doubao — and a growing list of third-party platforms that resell access. Build your prompt here, then paste it into whichever of those you use.

  

  

    What are reference images for?
    
References pin down what text alone can't: a specific product, person or style. Seedream 5.5 accepts up to 10 reference images and keeps the subject consistent with them, which is what makes product series and character work practical. If you set a reference count above, the generated prompt includes an editing brief telling the model to match them.
